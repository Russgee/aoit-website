What is Career and Technical Education (CTE)?

Career and Technical Education, often referred to as CTE, is an educational pathway that integrates academic knowledge with technical and employability skills. It goes beyond the traditional classroom environment, emphasizing real-world experiences, industry certifications, and work-based learning opportunities. Our CTE programs are designed to prepare students for careers in Web Development, Game Development and Information Technology.

At Academy of Innovative Technology, we have 3 state-approved Career and Technical Education programs. These programs allow us to prepare students for the careers of the future.
