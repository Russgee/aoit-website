What is Career and Technical Education (CTE)?

Career and Technical Education, often referred to as CTE, is an educational pathway that integrates academic knowledge with technical and employability skills. It goes beyond the traditional classroom environment, emphasizing real-world experiences, industry certifications, and work-based learning opportunities. Our CTE programs are designed to prepare students for careers in Web Development, Game Development and Information Technology.

At Academy of Innovative Technology, we have 3 state-approved Career and Technical Education programs. These programs allow us to prepare students for the careers of the future.

The New York State Career and Technical Education (CTE) Program Approval refers to the process through which educational programs in career and technical fields are evaluated and authorized by the state. This approval signifies that a CTE program meets certain standards and requirements set forth by the New York State Education Department (NYSED).

Our state-approved CTE programs ensure that we meet the established quality criteria. This provides assurance to students, parents, employers, and higher education institutions that the CTE programs meet the necessary standards for providing students with the knowledge, skills, and competencies required for their chosen career paths.
