Academy of Innovative Technology High School offers a diverse range of courses designed to meet the rigorous standards set by the state of New York and provide our students with a well-rounded education.

Our high school program aligns with the New York State graduation requirements, ensuring that our students fulfill the necessary coursework to obtain their diploma.

**Mathematics**: Our Mathematics department offers a comprehensive range of courses that build a strong foundation in mathematical concepts, from algebra and geometry to calculus and statistics. Students will develop critical thinking skills, problem-solving abilities, and a deep understanding of mathematical principles.

**English**: The English department fosters language proficiency, reading comprehension, and effective communication. Through our English courses, students will enhance their writing skills, engage in literary analysis, and explore a variety of literary genres, both classic and contemporary.

**Science**: Our Science department provides an exciting opportunity to delve into the world of scientific discovery. From biology and chemistry to physics and environmental science, students will engage in hands-on experiments, develop scientific reasoning, and expand their knowledge of the natural world.

**Social Studies**: The Social Studies department offers a comprehensive range of courses that explore the complexities of human societies, cultures, and historical events. Through courses such as U.S. History, World History, Economics, and Government, students will gain a deep understanding of the social, political, and economic forces that shape our world.

**Physical Education (Gym)**: Our Physical Education program emphasizes the importance of maintaining a healthy and active lifestyle. Through engaging physical activities, team sports, and fitness training, students will develop physical fitness, coordination, and teamwork skills.

In addition to these core subjects, our Academics section showcases a variety of other required classes and electives that contribute to a well-rounded education. From foreign languages and arts to technology and vocational courses, students have the opportunity to explore their interests and expand their horizons.

We are proud of our dedicated faculty, who are experts in their respective fields, and their commitment to creating an enriching learning environment. By clicking on each department in our Academics section, you will discover detailed information about the respective department.
