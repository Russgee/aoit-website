Advanced Placement (AP) courses are college-level courses offered to high school students. These courses are designed to provide students with the opportunity to engage in challenging coursework and gain college-level knowledge and skills while still in high school. AP courses are developed by the College Board, a non-profit organization, and are recognized by many colleges and universities in the United States and internationally.

At Academy of Innovative Technology High School, we offer the following Advanced Placement courses:

- AP Computer Science Principles
- AP Biology
- AP Language

Upon receiving the requisite score on the Advanced Placement exam, taken at the end of the course, students may be eligible to receive college credit.
