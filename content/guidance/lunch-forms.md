### School Meals
Students attending school in person will be offered free breakfast and lunch daily in school.

### Support School Funding
You do not need to apply for your child to receive meals. However, we do ask families to complete [Family Income Inquiry Form](https://www.myschoolapps.com/Home/PickDistrict), which helps schools receive money for their programs. Your school will send a copy of this form home with your child, or you can get it from your school’s main office.
