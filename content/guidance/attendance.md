title: Attendance
description: "Attendance information about the Academy of Innovative Technology High School"
---

Maintaining regular attendance is crucial for a successful high school experience for your child, and at Academy of Innovative Technology High School, we prioritize the importance of attendance. We encourage all students and parents to familiarize themselves with the following guidelines and procedures regarding attendance:

**Daily Attendance**: To be marked present, students must be in their correct classroom at the designated start time of each class. Attendance is taken for every subject class, during every period, every day. Merely being present within the school building does not qualify as attendance.

**Lateness**: In cases where students unavoidably arrive late to school, it is important to bring either a doctor's note or a signed note from their parent, which should include the parent's daytime telephone number.

**Absences**: Students are encouraged to make every effort to attend school daily. If a child is absent, they must bring a note from their parent on the first day they return to school. The note should include the parent's daytime telephone number for verification purposes. Students should show the note to all their classroom teachers to ensure it is taken into account when calculating their grade, and then leave the note in the main for inclusion in the child's biographical record.

**Extended Absences**: If a student will be absent for three or more days, it is essential to notify both the main office and the child's guidance counselor.

**Metro Cards**: At the beginning of each term, students are issued one Metro card. If a card is lost or destroyed, there is a 10-school-day wait period before a new card can be issued. Lost cards can be reported in the main office during the student's lunch period. Students will be required to show their school I.D. and program card for verification.

**Suspensions and Attendance**: In cases where a student is suspended, whether in-house or to an alternative site, parents will continue to receive the daily absence telephone call. Once the school receives confirmation that the child attended as required, their daily attendance record will be corrected accordingly.

**Biographical Updates**: It is crucial that we have accurate and up-to-date address and telephone information for all our students. Any changes in telephone numbers or addresses should be promptly reported to the main office, preferably accompanied by a utility bill as proof.

**Parental Inquiries**: Our school encourages parents to regularly call and confirm their child's attendance. It is best to make these inquiries between 10:30 a.m. and 1:30 p.m., and have the child's OSIS number (or NYC Student I.D. number) available. If requested, "Period Attendance" can be provided, informing parents of all the periods their child has missed during the current week.

At Academy of Innovative Technology High School, we believe that regular attendance lays the foundation for academic success and fosters a sense of responsibility. By adhering to our attendance guidelines and actively participating in your child's attendance management, we can work together to ensure a fulfilling and rewarding high school experience.
