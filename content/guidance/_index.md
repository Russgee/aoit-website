title: Guidance Department
description: "The Guidance Department at the Academy of Innovative Technology High School"
---

The mission of Academy of Innovative Technology’s School Counseling Department is to provide a proactive, comprehensive, and developmentally appropriate program to address students' academic and career goals in addition to supporting their personal and social needs. This is accomplished through a partnership with parents/guardians, staff, and community members to enable all students to become successful, productive, contributing citizens and lifelong learners in a diverse and changing world.
