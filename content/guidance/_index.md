---

 Our dedicated team of professionals are here to support and guide our students on their educational journey, personal growth, and future aspirations. We are committed to providing comprehensive guidance services that empower students to make informed decisions and thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.

The mission of Academy of Innovative Technology’s School Counseling Department is to provide a proactive, comprehensive, and developmentally appropriate program to address students’ academic and career goals in addition to supporting their personal and social needs. This is accomplished through a partnership with parents/guardians, staff, and community members to enable all students to become successful, productive, contributing citizens and lifelong learners in a diverse and changing world.

Here are some of the key services and resources offered by our Guidance Department:

**Academic Guidance**: Our counselors collaborate with students to develop personalized academic plans, ensuring students are enrolled in the appropriate courses to meet their educational goals. We provide guidance on course selection, credit requirements, and academic support programs to help students excel academically.

**Personal and Social Support**: We understand that personal challenges can impact a student's overall well-being and academic performance. Our guidance counselors offer a safe and confidential space for students to discuss their concerns, providing guidance on managing stress, developing effective study habits, and building healthy relationships. We can also connect students and families with external resources and support networks when needed.

**Individualized Counseling**: Our guidance counselors are available for one-on-one counseling sessions to address specific student needs. Whether it's academic struggles, social-emotional issues, or personal goal-setting, we are here to listen, provide guidance, and help students develop strategies for success.

We encourage all students and parents to take full advantage of the resources available in our Guidance Department. Whether you need assistance with academic planning, college applications, or personal challenges, our experienced counselors are here to provide guidance and support every step of the way.

To explore more about our services, resources, and upcoming events, please navigate through our Guidance section. We look forward to working closely with you to unlock your full potential and help you achieve your goals.
