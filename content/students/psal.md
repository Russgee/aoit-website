The mission of the Public Schools Athletic League is to provide opportunities for educating students in physical fitness, character development, and socialization skills through an athletic program that fosters teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship.



### Requirements to participate in Franklin K. Lane Knights Athletic Department

- PSAL Parent Consent Form 
- PSAL Physical dated back at most one calendar year
- 90% Attendance at school
- Unexcused absences prevent playing in games or practices
- 5 credits passed + Physical Education, and at least a 65% Average
- 3 Core classes passed (English, Social Studies, Science or Math)
